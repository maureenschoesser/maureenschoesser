## PhanganHousing
### A rental platform built around a local community need

[Visit PhanganHousing](https://phanganhousing.com)

### The challenge

While living on Koh Phangan, I saw how difficult and time-consuming it could be to find a home through rental offers scattered across Facebook groups and WhatsApp chats.

I wanted to create a simple place where people could find short-term and long-term rentals and connect directly with property owners.

### The solution

PhanganHousing brings rental listings together in a searchable platform. Visitors can filter homes by location, availability, property type, bedrooms and price.

Property owners can submit listings through a guided form, upload photos and manage their listings afterwards. The application also includes listing moderation.

### My role

The idea and product concept were entirely my own. I defined what the platform needed to do and directed its development.

ChatGPT and Claude helped me explore technical requirements and implementation options. Lovable provided the initial frontend draft. Further development involved Codex and direct code changes in GitHub.

I also challenged architectural recommendations when they did not fit the project's goals.

### A practical lesson in architecture and costs

One of my main goals was to run the project without recurring monthly costs. Within approximately two weeks, the initial photo delivery setup reached its cached egress allowance.

Working with Codex, I migrated photo storage and delivery to Cloudflare R2 while retaining Supabase for the database and authentication.

This experience taught me to assess AI recommendations against actual usage and operating constraints. Keeping recurring costs low remains a design priority.

### Tools and technologies

ChatGPT, Claude, Lovable and Codex supported development.

The application uses React, TypeScript, TanStack Start and Tailwind CSS, with Supabase for database and authentication services and Cloudflare Workers and R2 for hosting and photo storage.

### The outcome

A live community platform with rental search, listing submission, photo uploads and ongoing listing management.

The project demonstrates my ability to turn an observed need into a working product and adapt its architecture as practical constraints emerge.

## Feel to Heal with Me
### Connecting a service brand with the customer journey

[Visit Feel to Heal with Me](https://feeltohealwithme.com)

### The challenge

The project needed to bring different services and resources into a coherent experience. Visitors should be able to understand the offerings, explore retreats, find digital resources and take the next step towards an inquiry or booking.

### The solution

Feel to Heal with Me combines service pages, retreat information, online booking links, downloadable workbooks and other digital resources.

The implementation also includes newsletter subscription integrations and a client intake form with consent fields and server-side validation. Online session bookings link to Cal.com.

### My role

The idea and overall concept were my own. I developed the brand and strategy guide with ChatGPT and directed how the website should look, communicate and organise its offerings.

Lovable provided the initial frontend draft. I used Codex and Claude during further development, with most subsequent extensions implemented with Codex and additional changes made directly in GitHub.

My work centred on the concept, brand direction, service structure and decisions about how the different parts should connect.

### Tools and technologies

ChatGPT, Lovable, Codex and Claude supported development.

The implementation uses React, TypeScript, TanStack Start and Tailwind CSS, alongside Cloudflare Workers and D1 configuration, Cal.com booking links and MailerLite integrations.

### The outcome

A substantial service website connecting discovery, inquiries, external booking, digital resources and client preparation.

The project demonstrates how I translate a brand concept into a digital experience with several connected functions.

## Mindful HR
